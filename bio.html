Paul Fritz is a visual artist born in Morges, Switzerland. He lives and works in Lausanne. Paul completed a Master’s degree in Visual Arts at ECAL in Lausanne, after earning a Bachelor’s degree in Media & Interaction Design at the same institution.

In recent years, his work has been presented at PLATTFORM26 in KBCB, Bienne (2026), FOR in Basel (2025); Spazio Lampo in Chiasso (2025); Lokal.int in Bienne (2025); Treize in Paris (2025); Friendzone in Lausanne (2025); Art Genève (2025); City SALTS in Basel (2024); La Placette in Lausanne (2024); POP GUN in New York (2024); and HEK in Basel (2023). His films have been screened at LUFF in Lausanne, UFM in Lucerne, and HEK in Basel.

He is the recipient of the Fondation Leenaards Tremplin Prize in 2025 and the Walter + Eve Kent Grant in 2024.
